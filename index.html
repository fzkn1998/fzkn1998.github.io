Gantry Safety Monitoring

PSO Faisalabad Terminal · 7 bays, 4 cameras · deployed

Checks that workers on top of fuel tankers wear a helmet, harness and safety shoes and keep the harness hooked. A vision-language model double-checks every violation before it is logged.

YOLOv8Re-IDVLMFastAPIFlaskMLflow
Details
  • Detection zone per bay; each person is tracked across frames before a violation is raised, to cut false alarms.
  • Flask dashboard with live view, violation log by bay, bay analysis and CSV reports.
  • Reviewers correct boxes and add images to training data; retraining runs through a local MLOps pipeline with MLflow.
  • AI alerts also connected to the plant's SCADA system with alarms, snapshots and event logging.

2:57 · lorry entering and parking on the bridge

Weighbridge Alignment and Driver Check

Entrance weighbridge · existing CCTV camera

Makes sure the lorry stands fully on the bridge and the driver steps out before weighing, two checks the operator used to do by eye.

YOLOv8-segOpenCVPerspective transformTracking
Details
  • Segments the lorry and bridge plate, then maps the camera view to a top view to measure position.
  • Live status for the operator: Empty, Stop, Parked, and whether the lorry is centred.
  • Logs a timed violation if the driver stays inside while the lorry is on the bridge. No extra sensors needed.

8:22 · upload, processing and dashboard

SAR Maritime Intelligence

Sentinel-1 satellite radar matched with AIS data

Finds ships in radar satellite images and flags "dark vessels" that have switched off their AIS location signal.

PyTorchSAR imagery12-model ensembleAISLeaflet
Details
  • Tiles Sentinel-1 IW GRD scenes and runs an ensemble of 12 models with flip test-time augmentation.
  • Matches detections to AIS records by distance and time (500 m, ±120 min by default); unmatched ships are marked dark.
  • Dashboard with analytics, dark vessel list, map view, scene comparison and reports.
